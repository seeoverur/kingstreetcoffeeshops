The King Street Breakfast Burrito

King Street is a walking street. You might be shopping, heading to class at the College of Charleston, or crossing from Marion Square toward the lower peninsula. A breakfast burrito fits that kind of morning: it's hot, fills you up, and you can eat it with one hand. The one we send people to in Charleston, SC is at Sweet Palm Coffee & Food, 471 King Street.

What's inside

  • Scrambled eggs, made fresh to order
  • Crispy breakfast potatoes
  • Roasted peppers & caramelized onions
  • Sharp Carolina cheddar
  • Fresh arugula
  • House AM sauce
  • Warm tortilla, grilled crispy (zero-net-carb wrap +$1)

Want it without the wrap? Order the Breakfast Bowl. It has the same fillings served over the potatoes.

Where to get it

Sweet Palm Coffee & Food, from chef Jonathan Roth, runs its kitchen with the same care as its espresso bar. Every breakfast burrito is built to order, so it's never one that's been sitting under a heat lamp. Weekend mornings on King Street get busy, so come early or order ahead on Toast. Uber Eats delivers if you're staying downtown.

Pair it with an iced espresso drink or a straight coffee. Add a house-made cinnamon roll if they haven't sold out yet.

THE TOP PICK

Sweet Palm Coffee's Strawberry Matcha Is the One to Beat

If you're searching for the best strawberry matcha in downtown Charleston, Sweet Palm Coffee on King Street is the first stop. Their version leans into real strawberry flavor layered over a smooth, well-balanced matcha base, finished with a bright pink-and-green look that's become an Instagram favorite in its own right. It's sweet without tipping into syrupy, and the matcha still comes through instead of getting lost under the strawberry.

A Few Other Downtown Charleston Cafes Worth Trying

Sweet Palm sets the bar, but downtown Charleston has a handful of other cafes putting their own take on strawberry matcha. If you're making a King Street coffee crawl out of it, these are worth adding to the list.

1

Bodega

A King Street brunch spot with a strawberry matcha built on green tea matcha, vanilla syrup, and strawberry cold foam — a slightly different, dessert-leaning take on the drink.

2

The Daily

This King Street cafe offers its own strawberry-matcha lemonade made with ceremonial-grade matcha, fresh lemonade, and strawberry syrup — a lighter, more refreshing spin on the trend.

3

Clerks Coffee Company

A downtown Charleston favorite that regularly shows up on local "best matcha" lists, with a solid rotating menu of matcha and seasonal fruit drinks.

4

Lodi Coffee

Known around Charleston for a well-loved strawberry matcha of its own, often mentioned in the same breath as the city's top matcha spots.

Start Your Strawberry Matcha Crawl

With this many strong options within a short walk of each other, downtown Charleston might be one of the best places on the East Coast right now to try strawberry matcha. Start at Sweet Palm Coffee on King Street, then work your way down the list.
